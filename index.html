Monthly Budget Breakdown
Income: Your estimated monthly paycheck based on your settings
Bills: Your recurring bills (rent, utilities, debt payments)
Actual Spending: Real spending from your bank statements
Extra Available: Income minus bills minus spending = money you could use for debt
Suggested Payment: 60% of extra (keeps a safety buffer) to put toward debt
Spending Optimization
What it shows: Categories where you can cut spending by 25%
Data used: Your transactions from the last 6 months
Potential Savings: How much you could save monthly if you reduce that category
How to use: Take the savings and add it to your debt payment each month
Example: Save $99/month on Food & Dining = $1,188/year toward debt!
